My story

I used to think I was healthy. Then I couldn't climb a small hill.

I had a demanding corporate career in shipping, London and Singapore, and I thought I ate well and exercised. Then the symptoms came. A Graves' disease diagnosis, a doctor who said my thyroid had to go, and a question I couldn't stop asking: is there another way?

"You're not a victim in your disease. You can take control of your own health."

Rock bottom

Stuck at the bottom of the hill

I had to sit in the car while my family walked on without me. That was the moment I stopped ignoring the signs.

Wake-up call

There had to be another way

I wanted to keep my thyroid and work with my biology. Functional medicine showed me how.

Today

I help people do the same

I trained, left the corporate job, and built a practice around root-cause work. Now I help others find their way back.
