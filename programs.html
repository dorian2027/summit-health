Questions

Common questions

Do I need to stop seeing my doctor?

No. Keep your medical care in place. Your doctor manages diagnosis and any medication, and we work on nutrition and lifestyle alongside that. Never change or stop medication without speaking to your prescriber.

Do I need a diagnosis to apply?

Not necessarily. Many people who apply have symptoms that haven't been fully explained. The application helps us see whether I can support you.

How do I get started?

Complete the short application. If we're a good fit, you'll book a discovery call to talk through your situation and your options.

Not sure which one fits?

Apply, and we'll talk it through on your discovery call.
